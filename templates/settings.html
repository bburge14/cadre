      GitHub/GitLab OAuth apps only accept ONE exact, registered callback
      URL -- but the callback URL below is normally computed from whatever
      address is in your browser's bar at the moment, which can easily
      differ if you reach Cadre from more than one place (localhost, a
      LAN IP, Tailscale...). That mismatch is exactly what produces
      GitHub's "the redirect_uri is not associated with this application"
      page. Set this to whichever single address you actually registered
      with GitHub/GitLab to pin it, regardless of which address the click
      came from. Leave blank to keep the old per-request behavior.
